Sanitized case study

Cyber-Resilient Data Protection

Designed and operated recovery-led protection capabilities across disk, cloud, and tape, with immutable retention and restoration validation.

Challenge

Protect critical workloads against operational failure, accidental loss, and cyber-resilience risks while meeting retention requirements.

My role

Designed protection architecture, supported platform operations, coordinated recovery testing, and maintained documented restore procedures.

Approach

Applied tiered protection, immutable copies, recovery validation, and operational monitoring across enterprise backup and disaster-recovery services.

Outcome

Improved recoverability, governance, and confidence in restoration for business-critical operations without exposing customer or production details.

Technology scope: Veritas NetBackup · Cohesity · Veeam · Immutable Retention · Tape · Business Continuity · Disaster Recovery
